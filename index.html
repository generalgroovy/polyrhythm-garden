Each dot is one sixteenth note. Rings share the same pulse, but return to their start at different times. Try 5 against 8; rotate one ring to change the conversation.

Your pattern saves in this browser. Download a project to keep a portable copy. Audio starts only when you press Play; switching tabs stops it.
